    Good to know

    • One playthrough is enough. There are no difficulty settings and no mission replay; free roam continues after the story, and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable.
    • Cheats lock trophies. GTA VI's cheats only unlock after the credits, and using one disables trophies and saving for that session — keep trophy runs cheat-free.
    • No online trophies. All 55 are single-player, so the Platinum can never be lost to a server shutdown.
    • Story trophies cannot be missed. All 17 unlock automatically as their chapters, missions and character strands are completed.

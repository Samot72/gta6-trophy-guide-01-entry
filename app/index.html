    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough: about 200 hours by the launch guides' estimate, difficulty roughly 4/10, no online trophies and no difficulty setting to worry about. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. There is no mission replay, but free-roam continues after the story and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable — keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter anyway. Exactly one trophy is flagged potentially missable: Positive Mental Attitude, over a Chapter 1 meditation.
